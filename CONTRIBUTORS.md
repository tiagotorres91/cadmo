# Contributors

Cadmo is authored and maintained by **[Tiago Torres](https://github.com/tiagotorres91)** — the creator, and the maintainer who gates every merge and release.

Cadmo also grows through collaborators, credited here. This project follows its own
[collaboration protocol](docs/collaboration-protocol.md): a second developer works in pair with
their own AI, coordinating through issues, with the maintainer as the only gate.
