# Value gate — opening the Cadmo method to the public

*The method's own gate, filled in for real before this repository was built (July 2026). Kept here as a living example: this is what a gate looks like when it's not fiction.*

**Problem (measurable):** the method existed as internal practice — valuable, but invisible. In the AI-building space, unpublished practice has near-zero reputation value, and the window is closing: adjacent methods (Spec Kit, OpenSpec, BMAD) were compounding stars monthly while this stayed in a drawer.

**Why this solution:** open-core beats keeping it closed (in this space, guarding a method too long means someone ships a similar one first and owns the narrative) and beats selling it closed (no reputation → no buyers). Public priority + attribution licensing protects authorship better than secrecy: commits are dated, a vault isn't.

**Success metric (business):** the method becomes referenceable — measured by adoption signals (stars, `create-cadmo` downloads, real adopters reporting back) and by inbound interest in the paid layer (assisted implantation). Honest horizon: months, not days.

**Data readiness:** 🟢 — the material existed (internal guides, real production practice, one full client instance); the work was distillation and de-identification, not creation.

**Verdict:** GO — asymmetric bet: low cost (the material existed), high upside (reputation compounds), controlled risk (client work and pricing stay private).

**Value check (to revisit):** first review at +3 months — did anyone adopt it? Did the collaboration protocol resonate where it was shared? Kill-or-double-down applies to marketing effort, not to the repo (which costs nothing to keep open).
